# STAR in Four Stamps — treatment

**Style:** Mid-century Cartoon (Lemo-Opuscar `midcentury-toon`)
**Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 48 s, English narration + burned-in captions, −14 LUFS
**Source:** *Get the Offer* (Candidate Co.), ch. 04 "The STAR method" and its worked example (p. 7–8)
**Job:** teach one skill so the viewer can use it today, then send them to the book. A *how-to card*, not a story about a character.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Recipe card.** Four numbered steps S‑T‑A‑R, each a medallion that stamps onto an answer card, ending on the finished card. Clear, but it starts slowly: no reason to stop scrolling.
2. **Do / don't split.** The whole film as a split frame: the flat answer on top, the STAR answer below, line by line. Strong contrast, but in 9:16 two half‑frames make everything small, and the four steps get lost.
3. **Problem gesture → four stamps → the shape of a good answer.** Open on the question and the flat answer that fails (one gesture: a shrug, the answer card stays nearly blank and gets a red X). Turn: "Use STAR instead." Four steps, each a medallion stamped onto the same card by the candidate's hand, with the book's own example landing in a detail tag. Payoff: the four medallions leave the card and become a timing dial (15 / 10 / 50 / 25 %) that rotates into a four-point atomic star, the literal STAR. CTA on the book.

**Chosen: 3.** It has a hook in the first second (a question every viewer has been asked), one clear turn (same card, blank → full), and a payoff only this medium does: a real piece of information (how long each part should be) turns into an ornament. The do/don't split from option 2 lives inside it as one shot (the wrong answer).

## 2. Logline and arc

A nervous candidate's answer card stays blank until four stamped letters fill it, and the four letters become the star they're named for.

- **Setup (0–10 s):** the question stamps in; the candidate shrugs out the flat answer; split frame, red X: "That tells them nothing."
- **Turn (10–12 s):** silence. "Use STAR instead." The first medallion lands.
- **Development (12–36 s):** S, T, A, R. One hand action per step, on a beat. Each detail comes from the book's teamwork example.
- **Payoff (36–42 s):** the medallions fly into a dial, the dial shows the time split, and it rotates into a starburst.
- **Ending (42–48 s):** the candidate holds the finished card next to the book, dead still. "Every common question, broken down. Get the Offer. Link in bio."

## 3. Benchmark

- **1950s classroom films** (Coronet / EB style): learn direct address, one sentence per picture, numbered cards, and the calm authority of the narrator. Take none of their drawings, titles or music.
- **Short-form "one tip" Reels by career coaches:** learn the hook (the exact interview question on screen in the first second), one idea per video, and a CTA held long enough to screenshot. Don't take the talking head, the stock footage or trending audio.

## As built (changes from the first plan)

- **The candidate** wears a black suit, a white shirt, a red tie and black shoes (the user's call). Caption stars and bullets use mustard as the accent.
- **Timing:** the steps start at 13 / 19 / 25 / 31 s, the payoff at 37 s and the CTA at 43 s; the film is 49 s. The split frame comes in at 6.6 s so its label has time to be read.
- **No round call-outs:** in 9:16 the answer card fills the width, so each step's detail lands directly on its card row; the Action step gets the finger-led push instead.
- **Silence:** 10.0–10.75 s room tone only, then the narration of the turn; the music returns with the S stamp at 13.0 s.

## 6. Cue map

- 120 bpm swing, D major area. A cool-jazz quartet: vibraphone (comping and step chords), jazz pizzicato bass (walking), muted trumpet (melody, the "wah" droop on the X), flute (the payoff line), brushes (synthesised swish + snare taps) and finger snaps.
- 0–4: vibraphone chord stab on 0.25 (question pop), snaps on 2 and 4, bass enters at 2.0.
- 4–10: walking bass and brushes; muted trumpet answers the balloon; on 8.5 the trumpet droops (the X).
- 10–12: **silence** (room tone only), pips at 10.6, 10.9, 11.2, 11.5.
- 12–36: one bar of stamp per step, then two bars of groove. **Each step has its own vibraphone chord** on its stamp: S = Dmaj9, T = Bm9, A = Em9 (accented, held), R = A13.
- 36–42: the four step chords come back, one per medallion as it lands in the ring (36.5, 37.0, 37.5, 38.0); the flute takes the line; up a step to E major at 40.5 for the star.
- 42–48: half-time feel; vibraphone glissando into the final Emaj9 at 46.0; tails ring past the iris.

## 7. Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook 0–4 | warm room tone | pen write-on scratch, card pop | stab + snaps |
| Wrong 4–10 | room tone, mantel-clock tick on the beat | paper flap of the balloon, split-frame paper swish, rubber-stamp thud for the X | walking groove, trumpet droop |
| Silence 10–12 | room tone only, then nothing at 11.8 | pips for the empty circles | **none** |
| Steps 12–36 | room tone | damped rubber stamp on each medallion, paper swish on wipes, slide whistle on the finger tilt, tag pop | full combo, a chord per step |
| Payoff 36–42 | room tone | whooshes for the medallions, iris "shhk", sparkle ting | flute + recap chords, step up |
| CTA 42–48 | room tone | book thump, starburst ting | half-time, final chord |

Two near-silences: 10–12 s (before the turn) and a half-bar breath at 41.5 s before the CTA. Sound as a transition: the narration of each step starts before its iris (J-cut); the stamp tails and the final chord ring over the cuts (L-cut).

## 8. Type and captions

- Kicker in **Oleo Script Bold** ("Interview tip", "The fix"), titles and numerals in **Alfa Slab One** over an offset colour plate, body and captions in **Jost 500/600**.
- Caption card: a paper slab with an ink border and small turning stars, bottom-centre at y ≈ 1430–1520 (above the Reels UI), ~44 px Jost, one or two lines.
- Step header (top): an ink disc with a paper letter, "STEP n OF 4", and the step name.
- Detail tags: a paper card with a star bullet, popping on the stamp beat beside the call-out.
- Brand: "Get the Offer" and "Candidate Co." are the user's own; drawn in the film's own type (no copy of the PDF cover's fonts).
