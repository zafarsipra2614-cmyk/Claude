§3. Left-Digit Bias

Shoppers read a price from left to right, and the first digit sets the impression of how large the number is. A price of $9.99 is filed as “nine-something,” while $10.00 is filed as “ten,” even though the gap between them is a single cent. Because attention fades across the remaining digits, the decision is often made before the cents are read at all.
